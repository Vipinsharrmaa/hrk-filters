FREQUENTLY ASKED DISTRIBUTOR QUESTIONS

Straightforward terms for auto parts wholesalers, multi-brand workshops, and fleet maintenance teams.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (M.O.Q.) for wholesale dispatches?

expand_more
Our standard wholesale M.O.Q. is 1 Master Carton per individual SKU (typically 40 to 60 units depending on the filter dimensions). For new distributor test orders, mixed carton assortments across fast-moving 2-wheeler models can be arranged through your assigned relationship manager.

Can we request product samples for quality and OE fitment testing?

expand_more
Yes. We dispatch verified test sample kits containing our top 5 high-demand air filter papers and foam cartridges to established automotive trade counters and workshop owners. Contact our wholesale desk on WhatsApp with your GSTIN credentials.

How do wholesale pricing and distributor margins work?

expand_more
Pricing is structured into two tiers: Authorized Regional Stockist and Counter Wholesaler. Both tiers adhere to enforced Minimum Operating Price (M.O.P.) standards to protect your local retail margins. Login to the portal or speak with our sales desk to unlock official wholesale rate cards.

What is your standard shipping turnaround time across India?

expand_more
All standard SKU inventory is warehoused in our Delhi OEM corridor. Orders placed before 2:00 PM are handed over to major freight carriers (TCI, V-Trans, ARC, Safexpress) within 24-48 hours, complete with LR dockets and e-Way bills.

Do you provide OEM branding or custom filtration media specifications?

expand_more
Krishna Auto Agencies offers private-label contract manufacturing for fleet owners, corporate exporters, and brand aggregators. Custom pleat heights, synthetic blend papers, branded boxes, and special anti-corrosion finishes are supported.
